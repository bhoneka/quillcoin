"RUN VOID"

A run that ends in any other way than at its dungeon is thrown away: the flight was stopped, the module was
switched off, the flight came down early, the game closed. The hider does not get to go on from where the
run ended. The next run draws a new point.

   2026-09-29 03:07:07 UTC   no recording: the recorder stopped by itself - run void

Here the game closed by itself in the middle of a flight. That run hid nothing.

   2026-09-28 23:55:02 UTC   the server did not sign the book - nothing hidden

A test. No book was left in a chest.


COIN 307

Its recording has the line "recording saved" and no line "recording published". The tool records until the
hider has been pulled away from the chest by a pearl, and this one time it did not notice that he had been
(version 0.7.5 put that right). So this one recording was ended and published by hand.

It was replaced once, before the round opened: another player stood in view during its last seconds, and
those seconds were cut. The fingerprint on the board is the fingerprint of the file that is there now, and
since the round opened neither of them can be changed.


WHAT THIS LOG CANNOT PROVE

It is written by a program on the hider's own computer. It makes tampering visible; it cannot make it
impossible. That is why it is only one of four things that are published for every book, next to the
fingerprint of its code, the recording of its hide, and the source of the tool.
